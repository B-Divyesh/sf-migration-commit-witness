Policy / effective 2026-08-28

Privacy, without a fog layer.

The CLI runs locally and sends us nothing. The website uses no analytics, advertising, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

What the CLI handles

Migration Commit Witness reads the test database URL and signing key from environment variables. They are used in process and intentionally omitted from witness files. Command output is also omitted. The CLI makes no network request and has no telemetry.

What this site stores

If you restore or buy the Team rollout kit, the site stores the license token under sb_license:migration-commit-witness and a timestamped verification result in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is stored until you provide or return with a license.

License verification

The token is sent over HTTPS to Sociobot’s product verification endpoint. Verification occurs on first unlock and at most once per day afterward. Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, processes checkout and receipt information under its own obligations.

Your control

Clear this site’s storage in your browser to remove the local token and cached verdict. The free CLI and documentation continue to work without it. For privacy requests, use the support contact on your purchase receipt.
